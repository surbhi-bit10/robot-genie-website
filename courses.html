Core Marketing Syllabus

Digital Marketing Curriculum Highlights

A comprehensive look at the tactical pillars taught across our core digital marketing curriculum modules.

01

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Keyword research methodology, search intent mapping, on-page optimization, content structure, internal linking, and competitor search analysis.

02

Technical SEO

Crawlability, indexability, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonicalization, Core Web Vitals, mobile responsiveness, and schema markup implementation.

03

Google Ads

Campaign architecture for Search, Performance Max, Display, and YouTube Video ads. Smart bidding strategies, negative keywords, and quality score management.

04

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Meta Ads Manager, custom and lookalike audiences, creative testing matrices, conversion optimization, and dynamic retargeting funnels.

05

GA4 & Google Tag Manager

Event-driven data tracking setup, custom conversion events, trigger configuration in GTM, attribution models, and data exploration reports.

06

Content Marketing

Customer persona profiling, content calendar scheduling, blog strategy, copywriting principles, brand storytelling, and multi-format content distribution.

07

Email & CRM Automation

Lead segmentation, welcome sequences, automated nurture funnels, abandoned cart triggers, list hygiene, and deliverability optimization.

08

LinkedIn Lead Generation

B2B outreach strategies, executive thought leadership positioning, company page growth, sponsored content, and message ads targeting key decision-makers.

09

Freelancing & Agency Building

Client discovery calls, service proposal drafting, value-based pricing, retainer structuring, reporting templates, and scaling independent client work.
