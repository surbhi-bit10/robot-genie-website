About Robot Genie

An AI-powered career training institute based in Laxmi Nagar, Delhi, dedicated to real-world competence, practical campaign mastery, and career outcomes.

Our Story

Redefining Career Education for the AI-Driven Era

Robot Genie was founded in Laxmi Nagar, East Delhi, out of a clear realization: the modern workplace has evolved drastically, yet conventional training institutes continue to teach outdated syllabus slides without practical application.

Search algorithms, programmatic ad platforms, and artificial intelligence models are fundamentally restructuring how businesses acquire customers and manage operations. To succeed today, aspiring professionals do not just need theoretical definitions—they require hands-on proficiency with live tools, live budgets, prompt workflows, and performance metrics.

Located in the vibrant student and commercial hub of Laxmi Nagar, Robot Genie serves as a dynamic launchpad for ambitious learners. Our training floor combines modern digital marketing execution with generative AI automation, enabling students to graduate with verified portfolios, live project experience, and the confidence to excel from day one.

Methodology

Our Teaching Approach

Six foundational pillars designed to maximize individual comprehension, practical ability, and job readiness.

Live Projects

Work on authentic websites, live campaign setups, and real tracking configurations. You learn by deploying experiments and analyzing live feedback loops rather than reading static case studies.

Small Batches

We strictly cap batch sizes to guarantee high mentor-to-student interaction. Receive personalized feedback on assignments, code, ad copy, and campaign analytics without getting lost in crowded rooms.

Expert Mentors

Learn directly from active industry practitioners who manage paid ad budgets, search campaigns, and analytics infrastructure in their day-to-day professional careers.

AI Tools Training

Deeply integrate generative AI, prompt engineering, content automation frameworks, and analytical copilot systems into your daily digital marketing workflow to multiply your productivity.

Interview Preparation

Master technical assessments and behavioral interview techniques. Refactor your resume, optimize your LinkedIn profile, and participate in realistic mock interviews with constructive feedback.

Placement Support

Gain access to career advisory sessions, curated job openings from hiring partners, and ongoing guidance during applications until you land an exciting role in the digital space.
